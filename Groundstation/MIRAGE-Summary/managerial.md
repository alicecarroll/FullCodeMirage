# MIRAGE Project Management

## Team structure and workflow
MIRAGE is run as a student project under RiseUpp and Astronomisk Ungdom, with support from Uppsala University, SenseAir AB, and external calibration partners such as Norunda. The team is split across science, electrical, mechanical, outreach, and general project tasks, with voluntary students and thesis students sharing the workload.

The project uses weekly team meetings plus subteam meetings, with OnlineGantt for planning and Discord for internal communication. The current critical path is the chain from defining the scientific objectives through requirements, ordering materials, and building the pressurised chamber.

## Schedule and milestones
The schedule is structured around thesis work and the BX39 campaign timeline. Thesis students start from March and are expected to work full-time until early June, while volunteer team members keep the project moving until then. Important milestones in the current plan include PDR, CDR, integration and testing, and final launch preparation for October 2026.

## Resources and funding
The budget is being built from several sources:

- Uppsala University support for thesis projects
- the RiseUpp association budget and Astronomisk Ungdom grant opportunities
- SenseAir AB hardware sponsorship, including K96 sensors and IR lamps
- lab and thermal-vacuum access through Uppsala University and IRF
- support from Norunda for calibration gases and reference measurements

The SED estimates the hardware cost at roughly 10,970 SEK including margin, with total managerial costs and travel driving the overall project budget much higher. The hardware side is largely covered, but outreach and campaign costs still need sponsorship attention.

## Organisation practices
The team keeps the SED and related planning documents as living artefacts. Thesis students focus on defined work packages such as PCB design, data analysis, thermal system design, pressurised gas sensing, and calibration, while the voluntary team members mentor, integrate, and tie the subprojects together.

## Management risks to watch
The main management risks are recruitment delay, schedule pressure on the pressurised chamber, and the dependency of later work on early design choices. The team also needs to manage procurement lead times, keep the documentation current, and make sure the experiment remains within the budget and interface constraints while the design matures.
